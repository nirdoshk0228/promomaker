# Naukrani Nahi, Rajkumari Hoon Main — Show Bible

**Format:** Vertical micro-drama, 35 episodes, ~75–135 sec each
**Genre:** Royal family drama · hidden identity · revenge · romance
**Setting:** Suryagarh Haveli, a fictional royal palace near Udaipur, Rajasthan (present day)
**Language:** Dialogue in Hinglish (Roman Hindi); scene directions in English
**Original work:** all characters, places and events are fictional.

## Logline
A maid who has served the royal Suryagarh family all her life discovers she is their lost princess — the baby everyone believed died in a fire 22 years ago — and must expose the woman who set that fire before she is silenced too.

## Backstory (what really happened)
22 years ago Maharaja Vikram Singh, Maharani Gayatri and baby Meenakshi were at the old hunting lodge (Shikargah). Kaushalya and Bhanwar set it on fire. Vikram was declared dead, Gayatri was never found, the baby vanished. Kamla Bai found the baby in the bushes with the crested payal and raised her as her own daughter inside the haveli. Arjun's father, the accountant Mohan Rathore, saw too much and was framed for the fire; he died in jail.

## Season arc (episode beats)
1. Engagement party humiliation; Daadi Sa sees the payal.
2. Kaushalya threatens Meera; orders her removed.
3. Goons try to drown Meera in the baoli; Arjun saves her and sees the birthmark.
4. Tanya frames Meera for stealing a diamond necklace.
5. Arjun exposes Tanya with CCTV.
6. Devraj harasses Meera; she slaps him.
7. Kamla Bai reveals she found Meera as a baby; Bhanwar overhears.
8. Kamla Bai is poisoned; her last words point to the sealed Sheesh Mahal.
9. Meera finds her mother's portrait in the Sheesh Mahal.
10. Arjun reveals his father's story; Tanya photographs them.
11. Kaushalya forces the wedding to be moved up; Arjun agrees.
12. Daadi Sa gives Meera the key to the Maharaja's tijori.
13. Meera finds the Maharaja's will; Devraj burns it.
14. The family lawyer has been bought by Kaushalya.
15. Sangeet: Meera forced to dance; Tanya pushes her into the diyas.
16. Arjun saves her; Daadi Sa collapses calling "Meenakshi".
17. Daadi Sa demands a DNA test; Bhanwar bribes the lab tech.
18. Fake "no match" report; Arjun spots the bribe.
19. Arjun gets the lab tech's confession; Meera is kidnapped.
20. Devraj takes Meera to the burnt Shikargah and lights a match.
21. Arjun rescues her; Meera finds a recent letter signed "Gayatri".
22. Pushkar ashram: the sadhvi is Maharani Gayatri.
23. Gayatri tells the truth; mentions Arjun's father was there that night.
24. Arjun's father was a hero, not a criminal; Bhanwar's men attack the ashram.
25. Gayatri hidden in Arjun's hotel; Tanya finds her shawl in Arjun's car.
26. Arjun tells Tanya the truth; Tanya tells her mother "Gayatri is alive".
27. Kaushalya plans murder on the wedding day.
28. Meera is locked in the storeroom; Tanya opens the door.
29. Tanya hears her mother's plan and switches sides.
30. Bride swap: Meera under the ghoonghat in the mandap.
31. Gayatri enters the mandap; Devraj takes Daadi Sa hostage.
32. Arjun disarms Devraj; Tanya testifies; Bhanwar threatens to reveal more.
33. Bhanwar confesses; Kaushalya arrested but hints at "the real player".
34. Rajtilak of Rajkumari Meenakshi; Arjun proposes; a letter in the Maharaja's handwriting arrives.
35. Is Maharaja Vikram alive? Season cliffhanger.
